description: "Every Friday at 8:53am, a launchd job exports the trailing seven days of session data, computes the series metrics, appends them to a history file, and drafts that week's LinkedIn post for me to edit. Fixed definitions separate human sessions from bot sessions, and anonymization is a rule in the pipeline instead of a thing I have to remember. The draft still gets edited by hand; the pipeline just eats the part of Friday I shouldn't be spending on arithmetic."
