title: "There is no average session"
date: 2026-04-28
description: "95 sessions across five projects over 38 days: 72.6% lasted under 30 seconds, 13.7% ran past 15 minutes, and almost nothing lived in between. The long tail owns the budget too, with that 13.7% consuming 91.4% of the tokens. Quick checks and deep work are different activities that happen to share a tool. If you plan capacity around the mean, you're planning for a session that doesn't exist."
