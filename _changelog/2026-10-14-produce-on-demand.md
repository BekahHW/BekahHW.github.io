description: "A regulated-finance conversation raised three questions: does our session capture keep full tool arguments and results or only summaries, can an agent's actions be reconstructed in order, and could compliance rules run on top without changing capture? I pulled 111 sessions at full payload, nearly a gigabyte of JSON and 19,976 tool calls, and the answers were yes, yes, and mostly. A one-line query returned every action that touched a given path in September with its full input and output attached. 'Tests before push' held in 76% of applicable sessions. 'No edits after push' failed in 27, because iterating after a push is how people actually work; the rule was right and the policy was wrong. An absent step means not observed, never did not happen. What's missing is a stable id per action and a verdict field, which is product work rather than capture work."
