title: "Put the model on trial"
date: 2026-09-18
description: "We gave a frontier model two weeks of real engineering work, then staged an actual trial: prosecution and defense subagents arguing from the session records, a judge striking every claim the evidence couldn't support. The judge struck five, and three of them were us blaming the model for our own environment. The real verdict was that most of the model's work couldn't be judged at all, because an export failure ate the records. You can't grade a model on evidence you didn't keep, so fix the telemetry before you fix the model."
