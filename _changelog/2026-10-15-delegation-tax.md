title: "The delegation tax"

description: "What does it cost to tell the agent 'commit and push' instead of typing it yourself? Across 1,504 sessions and 11,300 user turns, 374 turns were purely mechanical asks: commit, push, open the PR, merge, run the tests. They cost $921, about 3% of spend, median 94 cents each. The ask is nearly free; the context is not. A plain 'push' turn carries a median 272k tokens of history, and 98.7% of the input tokens on those turns are the conversation being re-sent. The same asks in a fresh context would have cost about a quarter as much. The outliers are the agent deciding what 'yes push' meant: one of those ran 597 tool calls and cost $98.93. Fifty-seven of the 374 were the last turn of their session, the closing ritual."
