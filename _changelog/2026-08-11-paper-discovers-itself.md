description: "We pointed paper at our own fleet: three weeks of session records, run through an adversarial refute-everything review. The budget mostly goes to re-reading. Input tokens outnumbered output 375 to 1, one in four model calls was a permission check re-reading a huge cached prefix, and the single most common prompt in the whole fleet was the harness asking 'where were we?'. Also, there is no average session; a handful of whales carry most of the spend. Turns out the agent's biggest expense is remembering what it was doing."
